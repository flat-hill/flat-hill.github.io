Regulatory snapshot requiring current verification

FCC and domestic content

The published source set includes FCC DA 26-786 and the referenced domestic-end-product rules. The general FAR test requires U.S. manufacture and domestic component cost exceeding 65% through 2028, then exceeding 75% from 2029, with applicable exceptions.

Check current primary rules and the finished SKU. Fixed industrial arms and mobile platforms can have different scope. Normal equipment authorization is separate. No MATTER POISE FCC approval, legal eligibility determination or unconditional Made in USA claim is established. Do not infer permanent chip exemptions; FTC origin claims have a separate standard.
