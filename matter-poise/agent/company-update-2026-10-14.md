# MATTER POISE — management clarification

As of 14 October 2026. These are management-confirmed statements for the public investor briefing, not an independent legal, technical or commercial audit.

## Company stage and orders
MATTER POISE is newly established and currently has no orders. This statement does not establish whether revenue, evaluation discussions or other commercial activity exists; those are separate questions for the team.

## Current manufacturing and the U.S. production plan
Joint modules are currently predominantly manufactured in China and imported into the U.S. The company plans to establish U.S. joint-module production lines gradually. Those lines are a future milestone, not present installed capacity.

## Joint-module capability and IP milestone
Management reports established joint-module technical capability supported by completed technical drawings. Relevant joint-module patent/IP assets are being prepared for placement in the U.S. company, with completion targeted before the pre-A financing round. This does not state that placement is already complete or specify patent grant status, patent numbers, a transfer mechanism or completed freedom-to-operate analysis. A controlled asset schedule and relevant documentation should support detailed review.

## Why multiple platforms share an engineering base
The arm, spherical and quadruped platforms use shared large-joint modules and a common hardware architecture. Reusing this engineering base is intended to limit duplicated R&D and support different customer applications. It is not a measured reuse percentage or a claim that all parts are common. Each platform still needs its own integration, control, testing and validation.
