How the compliance and IP path fits the roadmap +

Classify and authorize

Assess each finished product under the FCC’s advanced-robotic-device definition. Fixed industrial arms and mobile platforms require different scope analysis. Normal equipment authorization is a separate release task.

Manufacture and document

U.S. supplier spending is not the domestic component-cost test. Qualifying manufacture and component evidence must support each SKU. A >75% supplier-spend objective alone cannot prove compliance.

Secure the rights package

Complete chain of title, third-party license review, U.S. filing strategy and product-specific freedom-to-operate work. U.S. financing history and a patent filing do not independently establish FTO or FCC eligibility.

ESP32 support in Meta’s Muse SDK is a technology example, not evidence that a chip or finished robot can never be restricted. No regulatory approval is claimed for MATTER POISE.
