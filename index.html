THE PHYSICAL FOUNDATION
OF INTELLIGENT MACHINES

Different physical forms.
One standard for capable action.

Matter Poise develops robotic systems for real-world movement, manipulation, sensing, control, and deployment. Our work spans multiple robotic forms, but the engineering goal is consistent: build machines that can operate reliably beyond the laboratory.

Matter Poise is based in Georgia, U.S.
