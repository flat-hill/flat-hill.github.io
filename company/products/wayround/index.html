THE SYSTEM, CONSIDERED TOGETHER

Designed around
the physical task.

WAYROUND explores spherical mobility as a platform for movement, sensing, and inspection. System configuration depends on the environment, access needs, sensing requirements, communications, and intended task.

01

An enclosed mobility architecture.

The spherical form brings the shell, internal actuation and stabilization into one mechanical system. Operating surfaces and obstacles are part of the configuration review.

02

Start with what must be observed.

Define the sensing task, field of view, communications and mounting interfaces before selecting the payload. Illustrated accessories are configuration examples.

03

Conditions make the measurement.

Runtime, speed, protection and obstacle performance depend on the configuration and test conditions. The R700 reference retains design targets and an unresolved source discrepancy.
