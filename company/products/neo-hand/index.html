Version notes & interpretation

Values are transcribed from the selected source version, not independently verified acceptance specifications. Confirm the exact hardware revision, operating conditions and availability with the team.

Pinch force and enveloping grasp force are different metrics; neither is a payload rating.

The selected August presentation states 900 g, 20 N pinch, 60 N grasp and 320 Hz. Other promotional materials contain different values; they are not merged into this version.
