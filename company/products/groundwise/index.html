THE SYSTEM, CONSIDERED TOGETHER

Designed around
the physical task.

Development focuses on balance, terrain interaction, mobility, payload integration, control, serviceability, and reliable operation in real-world conditions.

01

The route shapes the configuration.

Terrain, surface transitions, clearance and the expected duty cycle belong in the mobility discussion. Legged and wheel-legged configurations must be evaluated separately.

02

A platform for the task.

Compute, sensing, communications and payload interfaces are selected around the application. The technical reference lists configuration-specific interfaces and optional compute.

03

Think beyond the first run.

Access to joints, wheels, wiring and replaceable assemblies matters throughout the operating life. Confirm the service package and acceptance conditions for the selected configuration.
