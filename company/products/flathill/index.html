THE SYSTEM, CONSIDERED TOGETHER

Designed around
the physical task.

FlatHill is Matter Poise’s brand for dexterous manipulation end-effectors. Finger geometry, joint articulation, contact surfaces, mounting, power, controls, and host-system integration are considered as parts of one connected mechanism.

01

The point where robotics meets matter.

Object geometry, contact surfaces and the intended grasp guide the choice of end-effector. Pinch force and enveloping grasp force describe different interactions.

02

Small movements begin at the joint.

Compact rotary drives create the mechanical foundation for finger articulation. M01, M01 Neo and M02 have distinct reference configurations and remain available in the component catalog.

03

Part of a connected machine.

Mounting, power, communication and host control must be defined together. Coordinated two-hand applications also require a separately specified arm and control system.
