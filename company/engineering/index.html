ENGINEERING VIEW / JOINT ARCHITECTURE
THE WHOLE OPERATING LIFE

A machine is more
than its first motion.

We approach physical systems with the full operating life of the machine in mind — from interfaces and integration to evaluation, serviceability, and continued product improvement.

Our team brings experience in robotic hardware solutions, cost control and standardized manufacturing from prior programs. That experience informs how we design mechanisms and prepare the next build.

01 / Engineer

Design for the whole system.

Bring mechanical, electrical, controls, interfaces, serviceability, and application requirements into the same conversation.

  • Self-developed micro and large-joint architectures
  • Component selection and cost-aware design
  • Assembly access, tolerance and service requirements
02 / Integrate

Bring the system together.

Mechanical, electrical, controls, sensing, and application requirements are considered as connected parts of one machine.

  • Mechanical mounting, wiring and communication
  • Configuration and revision management
  • Calibration, diagnostics and interface checks
03 / Evaluate

Learn from real use.

Testing, serviceability, operating feedback, and revision control inform continued product improvement.

  • Configuration-specific acceptance conditions
  • Build and test records
  • Service feedback and revision decisions
MANUFACTURING THINKING

Buildability
belongs in the design.

Our manufacturing approach connects component identification, assembly, testing and service information. The goal is a build that can be understood, evaluated and repeated.

01

Make the build identifiable.

Define part numbers, BOM revisions, incoming inspection requirements and assembly documentation together.

02

Give the result a record.

Connect the build configuration with calibration, functional checks, acceptance criteria and traceable records.

03

Design for the next revision.

Use assembly observations, service access and operating feedback to guide changes in the mechanism and process.

PRODUCT READINESS

Buildability, serviceability and real-world evaluation belong in the product from the beginning. Project scope, release criteria and production arrangements are established with the team.
