MANUFACTURING / BUILT INTO THE DESIGN

The first build
is just the beginning.

We approach hardware with the next build in mind: component choices, assembly steps, test methods and the cost of a good unit.

ENGINEERING VIEW

Look inside.
Think ahead.

Repeatability begins with the architecture, long before a production line.

01 / PROCESS

Design for the build.

Bring mechanical, electrical and manufacturing decisions into the same conversation.

  • Component selection and cost structure
  • Assembly access and interface definition
  • Design reviews and revision control
02 / PROCESS

Prove the process.

Use pilot builds to learn where quality, time and cost are won or lost.

  • Assembly and calibration instructions
  • Application-specific acceptance tests
  • Build records and corrective actions
03 / PROCESS

Scale what works.

The planned U.S. manufacturing path starts with qualified partners and progresses toward dedicated assembly, calibration and test cells.

  • Demand-led capacity decisions
  • Supplier and component traceability
  • Automation of stable, validated processes
THE LONGER VIEW

Toward lights-out
manufacturing.

Our roadmap connects ROSE with increasingly automated U.S. production. Expansion is intended to follow validated demand, yield and process reliability. A completed lights-out factory is not being claimed.
