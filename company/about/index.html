ABOUT MATTER POISE

Matter,
poised to act.

We develop intelligent machines for the physical world — machines that must move, manipulate, endure, and perform reliably in real environments.

01 / MATTER

The physical world.
And what truly matters.

Materials, mechanisms, motion and real operating conditions. We work at the physical foundation of intelligent machines.

02 / POISE

Balance. Control.
Readiness to act.

Poise is not only how we work. It is what we want our machines to become: capable, reliable, controlled, and ready for meaningful use.

OUR PERSPECTIVE

Matter creates possibility.
Poise moves it forward.

Physical AI is still early — not only technologically, but conceptually. Many future forms, workflows, and applications have yet to be defined. We believe that is an opportunity to build thoughtfully.

Matter Poise works where form, mechanism, mobility, manipulation, integration, buildability, testing and reliability come together. Our products are developed to be adapted and integrated into real work.

Matter Poise is based in Georgia, U.S.
